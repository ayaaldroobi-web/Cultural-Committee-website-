About us

The Cultural Committee was started many years ago- a group of students who were passionate about culture and interested in learning more about it,
and who wanted to support the Cultural Prefects in organising the school-wide celebrations for different cultures.
Since then, it has become a big part of the behind-the-scenes action we see in the cultural events we celebrate at WEGC.
We get many applications to the committee, as many students are keen to help out and organise events.
Our main jobs as a committee include decorating the school prior to a celebration week, planning the logistics and organising many lunchtime and breaktime activities that celebrate all aspects of culture (such as food sales and foreign language movie screenings), setting up for said events prior to their commencement, and creating social media posts that raise awareness about different cultures and their traditions and customs.
When we have a meeting, we assemble in the library usually during lunchtime.
The Prefects brief the committee on the upcoming event, and ask for suggestions and help with the organisation.
If you have any suggestions, ideas, and/or cultural celebrations and events you want to see celebrated at East, contact us!
