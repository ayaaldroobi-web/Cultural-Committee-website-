Welcome to Cultural Committee

Kia ora, Ni Hao, Bonjour, Hallo, Namaste, Marhaba, Bula, Hola, Konnichiwa, Salam, and Hello everyone! Welcome to the Cultural Committee!
We are devoted to organising and running fun events and activities that share, uplift, and spread awareness, understanding, and appreciation of all the 70+ cultures at Wellington East Girls College.
We love to connect people through different aspects of culture, including food, language, and attire!
We aim to create a more culturally aware and understanding school community through these events.
Here, you can learn more about what we do, who we are, and our goals!
