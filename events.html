Recent Events

Our most recent event was Cultural Diversity Week. This week is our main event, which takes place annually. Every day included an activity that promotes and celebrates different cultures. This year it took place in Week 7 of Term 2 (2nd - 5th July) Monday, unluckily, was a public holiday this year.

Tuesday

Short movies

Information

On Tuesday, we held a screening of a range of short movies displaying aspects from different cultures, in M16.
We also offered Fruit Bursts as a snack for those watching.

Wednesday

Cultural Attire Day

Information

Wednesday was our Cultural Attire Day.
Everyone was welcome to wear their formal cultural attire to school in celebration.
There were some beautiful outfits to be seen, and we all took a picture on the school's reception stairs.
We also, in collaboration with the International Committee, had a language swap activity.
For this, we got the help of our multilingual students and our international students to teach how to say phrases such as
'Hello', 'How are you?' 'Nice to meet you', and 'I love you', in Te Reo, Spanish, Japanese, German, Chinese, French, and Hindi.

Thurday

Food Sale

Information

On Thursday we ran a Food Sale in cooperation with the International Committee. There was lots of delicious food on sale from all corners of the globe. We were selling Kiwi sausage sizzles, Chinese dumplings, German potato pancakes, Japanese onigiri, Thai pad thai, and a Sri Lankan drink. All of our profits are going to the Women's Refuge Charity at the end of 2026.

Friday

Games Day

Information

'Games Day' was our activity on Friday. We hosted a Kahoot, made up with a collection of questions crafted by our committee members, as well as ran some cultural games, such as Pisti (a Turkish card game), Stadt Land Fluss (a German categories game), and Karem (a South Asian version of pool).
